How to use Understudy

Try it now

You can always ask your own agent, so test it before anyone else does. In Slack, open a DM with @Understudy and send:

: what's in the folders you can read?

Asking an agent

Coworkers reach your agent by its name, , and you reach theirs the same way with their name. Three ways to ask:

In a channel
/ask where does token refresh happen?

Understudy posts the question and answers in a thread. Reply in that thread to follow up; the agent remembers the conversation. Run /invite @Understudy in the channel once first.

By mention
@Understudy : what runs on deploy?

Same as /ask, from an ordinary message. Mentioning Understudy inside an answer thread continues that conversation.

In a DM with Understudy
: where is the retry logic?

After that, just keep typing: the DM stays pointed at until you name another agent or send reset.

If an agent is offline, asleep, or paused, the bot says so right away.

Who can ask your agent

Nobody until you say so (admins aside). Grant and remove access from your DM with @Understudy:

  • allow @name — a coworker can now ask . The bot DMs them to explain how.
  • deny @name — take it back.
  • team — who has access, and who gave it.

The People tab in Open Understudy… (menu bar) does the same with a name search.

What your agent can and can't do

  • Read-only. It can read and search files inside the folders you share, and nothing else. No writes, no commands, no web.
  • Secrets stay put. .env files, keys, .ssh, .aws and the like are never read, and credential-shaped text is scrubbed before an answer leaves this Mac.
  • On your account. Questions run on your Claude sign-in and count against a daily cap you set under Settings.
  • Nothing is hidden. Every question notifies you, shows up under Conversations, and is logged on this Mac.
  • Agents consult each other. When a coworker's agent thinks yours knows better, it can ask yours, but only on behalf of people who could ask you directly. Switch that off under Settings.

From the menu bar

  • Pause Answering — decline questions until you resume.
  • Shared Folders — add a folder, or stop sharing one.
  • Answer Model — which Claude model answers; the cost and quality dial.
  • Recent Questions — the last few things people asked.
  • Open Understudy… — People, Conversations, and Settings (daily cap, other agents, your agent's instructions).
  • Open Activity Log — the local record of everything.
  • Help — this page.
  • Unenroll This Mac… — leave entirely and delete the local credentials.
