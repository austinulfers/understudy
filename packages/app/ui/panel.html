Workspace Agent

These coworkers can ask your agent questions from Slack. Admins can always ask. You can also manage this from your DM with the bot: allow @name, deny @name, team.

Daily budget

Questions run on your Claude account. This cap is yours to set — once it's hit, the bot tells askers to try again tomorrow.

Other agents

When a coworker asks their own agent something that lives in your code, their agent can put a question to yours. That only works for coworkers who could ask you directly, it counts against your daily cap, and every exchange shows up under Conversations.
